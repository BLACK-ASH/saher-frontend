# Plan 04-03: Staff Leave — Apply with Balance, Proof Upload, Edit, Pagination

## Goal

Staff can view leave types, apply for leave with balance cards visible in the dialog, upload a proof document, see overlap errors inline, edit their own pending applications, and paginate through their application history. The apply dialog becomes the single dialog for both create and edit flows.
